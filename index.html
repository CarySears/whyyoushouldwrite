You don't need permission. Just a page to play with.

Almost everyone who wants to write carries the same handful of doubts. This class is built to quiet them, not answer them, just quiet them long enough to let you start.

"I'm not a real writer."
"I don't know where to start."
"No one wants to hear this."
None of that is required to begin. Just you, your story, and our support.
What you'll learn and experiment with

Over the course of one hour, live with Anna and Vindy.

✎

Cultivating a creative mindset to establish and nurture your writing practice.

✧

Support for overcoming writer's block, whatever shape it takes for you.

❦

Neutralizing your inner critic and letting your self-expression flourish.

☀

Trusting the power and truth of your own voice.

All you need to bring

Paper and a pen, or a blank doc on your computer. And an open mind. That's the entire requirement. Come ready to play.
